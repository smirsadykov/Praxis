The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content and no paid content or features.

2. Purpose and target audience
Ergon is an offline study companion for math, physics and engineering. It is for upper high-school and university students, and for engineers and self-learners who want to refresh the fundamentals. The problem it solves: textbooks and answer keys often show results but skip the reasoning, so learners get stuck. Ergon explains every step of each worked example, explains every symbol in every key formula, corrects common misconceptions, and lets the learner practise with instant answer checking. It covers 180+ topics from arithmetic to graduate level (calculus, linear algebra, quantum mechanics, relativity, control systems, circuits, mechanics), with 690+ worked examples and 3,700+ practice problems.

3. How to access the main features
No login is needed. Launch the app → choose a subject tab (Mathematics, Physics, Control, Electrical, Mechanical, Computer Sci) → tap any topic card. A topic page contains: a short article, key terms, diagrams, key formulas with every symbol explained, worked examples with "why this step" notes, and practice problems. For a practice problem, type an answer (a number, fraction or expression) and tap Check; "Show answer" reveals the answer and the explanation. The back arrow at the top left returns to the subject list. Progress badges on the topic cards show how many problems were solved.

4. External services
None for the core functionality. All lessons, examples and problems are bundled in the app, and it works fully offline. The only network use: some topics show freely licensed photos and illustrations from Wikimedia Commons, loaded directly from commons.wikimedia.org when the device is online (the author and license are shown under each image). No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no AI services and no payment processing.

5. Regional differences
None. The app works the same in all regions where it is available. The interface and content are in English.
