## Description (both stores)

Ergon teaches math, physics and engineering the way good tutors do: show a carefully worked example, explain why each step works, then let you try.

FROM BASICS TO GRADUATE LEVEL
• Mathematics: arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry, calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, real and complex analysis, abstract algebra, topology
• Physics: kinematics, Newton’s laws, energy, waves, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, relativity, quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, astrophysics
• Engineering: control systems, electrical circuits, mechanical engineering, computer science
• 180+ topics, each tagged basic, intermediate, advanced or graduate

LEARN BY WORKED EXAMPLES
• 690+ worked examples, every step labelled with what it does and why
• Plain-language intro to every topic: what it is, why it exists, the big idea
• Key formulas with every symbol explained: where it comes from and when it applies
• Common misconceptions, corrected head-on
• Diagrams, a glossary of the words you’ll see, and links between topics

PRACTICE THAT CHECKS YOU
• 3,700+ practice problems
• Type your answer and tap Check: numbers, fractions and scientific notation are understood
• Explanations for problems, and progress tracking per topic

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Everything is inside the app and works without internet
• Your progress stays on your phone
