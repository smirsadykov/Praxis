Support

Questions, ideas, or found a mistake in a problem? Write to easy.going.technologies@gmail.com. Please include the topic and problem number. We usually reply within two working days.

How do I type an answer?

Type a number or a short expression and tap Check. Numbers are compared with a small tolerance, so 0.333 and 1/3 style answers both work where it makes sense. If the checker doesn't accept a correct answer, tap Show answer and compare.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All lessons, examples and problems are inside the app. Only the optional photos from Wikimedia Commons need an internet connection.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
