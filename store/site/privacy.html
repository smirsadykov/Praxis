Privacy Policy

Ergon · Easy Going Technologies LLC · effective November 21, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Ergon has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own. Your progress (which topics you opened and which problems you solved) is stored only on your device.

Images from Wikimedia Commons

Some topics show freely licensed photos and illustrations from Wikimedia Commons. When such a topic is open and you are online, your device downloads those images directly from Wikimedia's servers. Like any website, Wikimedia receives your IP address and basic device information for that request; it is governed by the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy. We do not send Wikimedia any information about you.

Purchases

Ergon is free. It does not use any payment, advertising or analytics service. If paid features are added later, purchases will be handled by Apple or Google, and this policy will be updated first.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and all of its data is removed from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
